# Living Intelligence / Human Signal

Implemented from the owner-approved design brief and image in `../docs/`. The reference is a brand board: its palette swatches and type specimens are not public page content.

Use charcoal `#171916`, ivory `#F4EBDC`, amber `#D7A35D`, and muted teal `#6F9991`. Playfair Display regular and italic provide the editorial voice; Source Sans 3 provides readable supporting text. Licensed font subsets and OFL notices are under `public/fonts/`.

The homepage uses a four-link nameplate header, live two-line hero, static organic sculpture, ivory five-column work section, personal portrait, and selected research. The work row becomes two columns and then one. Existing archives, project pages, sources, and public resume remain available.

## Generated artwork

Created with the built-in image-generation tool using the approved image as a style reference. Final optimized assets are under `public/images/identity/`; the original generated files remain in Codex's generated-images directory. These are conceptual decorations, not research evidence, book covers, or documentary stills.

Shared prompt direction: standalone abstract artwork without text or people; matte charcoal, warm amber, ivory highlights, and muted teal; fine computational filaments, paper-like organic material, tactile grain, and warm directional light. Exclude neon, brain icons, globes, and generic network spheres.

- `living-intelligence-hero.webp`: wide asymmetric topological membrane draped through layered sheets, intricate triangular lattice and amber nodes, translucent teal surfaces; calm empty left and detailed sculptural right. A 960px mobile derivative preserves the artwork.
- `work-siml.webp`: porous organic material with irregular oval apertures and amber-lit struts.
- `work-bestie.webp`: delicate translucent membrane and fine radial filaments fanning from a soft amber fold.
- `work-books.webp`: layered pages unfolding in a sculptural fan, with amber edges and ivory surfaces.
- `work-film.webp`: projected amber light through vertical slits onto a tactile dark surface.
- `work-talks.webp`: repeating arcs of paper-like ribbons with amber-lit edges.

All typography on the website remains semantic HTML. Real book covers and documentary artwork remain on their individual pages.
