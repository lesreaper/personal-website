## Predicting useful structure

This note examines V-JEPA 2 through the lens of embodied simulation. It focuses on the distinction between predicting compressed representations and reconstructing every detail of an image.

Richard considers how prediction-first systems might relate to SIML's research questions. The piece is a reading of public research and a discussion of possible connections, rather than a claim to have developed V-JEPA 2.
