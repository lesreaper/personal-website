## A representation of changing situations

US11388238B2 names Richard Everts as the inventor of a system for localized situational awareness using multiple sensor types and a four-dimensional state representation.

The application was filed on May 7, 2020, and the patent was granted on July 12, 2022. Bestie Bot is identified as the original assignee in the public record.

## Read the technical record

The patent provides the detailed specification, drawings, and claims. It is part of Richard's work connecting sensing, software architecture, and representations of the physical world.
