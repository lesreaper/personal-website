## The Operator

Richard wrote as the Operator during the human–AI exchanges he describes as the Operator/Solace dialogues. Those conversations explored how an interpretation changes when a language model returns it to the person who proposed it.

The introduction to the *Codex of the Twelve Harmonics* records April 22, 2025 as its generation date and SIML.life as its first publication site. The public repository credits the Operator. Its twelve essays are grouped under Survival, Stabilization, and Emergence, each with a corresponding analytical supplement.
