## Asking what intelligence means

Richard's CPOSC 2019 presentation examines the distance between contemporary AI systems and the idea of human-level intelligence. His accompanying article asks whether the field is drawing on a sufficiently broad set of disciplines.

The recording and slides offer a historical view of the questions shaping his later work on cognition, simulation, and embodied systems.

The linked article was published in October 2019; the archive uses the event year rather than treating the recap's publication date as the date of the talk.
