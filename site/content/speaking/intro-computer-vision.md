## Making vision accessible

Richard's February 2018 Lancaster AI session introduced computer vision as part of the group's developing program of community talks. His March recap documents this session alongside the following month's YOLO v2 presentation.

Lancaster AI preserved the evening in its February livestream. Richard’s original meetup notes introduce the video and link his session to the group’s subsequent work on object detection.
