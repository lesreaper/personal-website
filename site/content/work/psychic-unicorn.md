## A different kind of AI interface

Richard launched Psychic Unicorn AI in beta with AI-assisted birth-chart analysis and a conversational question interface. It brings his engineering work into a consumer setting centered on personalized interpretation.

The product sits alongside his research and writing as an exploration of the interaction between systems, stories, and the people using them. It demonstrates a different application context from spatial sensing or simulation research.
