## A place for interpretation

Richard founded Psychic Unicorn and serves as its lead AI engineer. He built a consumer application where a person can start with a question, a tarot card, a birth chart, or a dream. People can keep a journal, explore the traditions behind a reading, and return to their own reflections. [Visit Psychic Unicorn](https://psychicunicorn.com)

The project raises a question that also runs through Richard’s philosophical writing. How can an AI help a person find language for an experience while leaving the interpretation in their hands?

## The 2025 beta

The first public beta paired birth-chart analysis with a conversational interface for follow-up questions. Richard’s launch announcement documents that early version. The current product brings additional practices, including tarot and dream exploration, into a shared space for reflection.
