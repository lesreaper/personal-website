## Across a country

A national conversation can make people sound interchangeable. On the road, every stop brought a different voice. The interviews took place in the participants’ own languages, across differences of faith, culture, and geography. Bringing those voices together was the work of the film. [Official trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=td1pxNXNjjU) · [Distributor’s release account](https://janson.com/news/a-fathers-tireless-efforts-bring-encouragement-to-families-affected-by-autism-in-new-documentary)

## Behind the camera

I directed and edited the documentary and produced it with Sugey Cruz-Everts. Rene Duran was the cinematographer and Jamie Marshall the art director. The American Film Institute lists the theatrical version at 93 minutes and records its New York opening on August 9, 2013. [AFI production credits](https://catalog.afi.com/Film/69624-THE-UNITED-STATES-OF-AUTISM)
