## What survives compression?

At Deep SIML Labs, I’m interested in what happens between a mathematical description and a machine running it. A compact representation can fit a larger experiment in memory. Decoding it also has a cost. The next comparison needs to account for both. SIML Studio lets people examine representations and check what comes back after a round trip. [Explore Studio](https://www.siml.life/studio) · [Published comparisons](/research)

## What would count as muscle?

A finger can shorten against a load, then relax. That motion gives us a reference for the Replicant research direction. Could an alternative mechanism reproduce some useful part of it? Ionogel and electro-fluid concepts belong to this inquiry. The near-term work is to compare a reference model with observations and establish what a convincing physical test would measure. [Functional biological models](https://www.siml.life/biological-systems) · [Materials program](https://www.siml.life/materials)
