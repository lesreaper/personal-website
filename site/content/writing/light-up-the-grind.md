## What the work is for

Light Up the Grind is Richard's short memoir about fulfillment and the lessons that emerge from both success and failure. It turns toward the lived experience behind a public body of work: ambition, effort, and the search for a meaningful life.

Published by BookBaby in 2022, the book is recorded as a 64-page work. Its compact form gives these reflections a direct, personal focus.

## Alongside the projects

The book offers a personal counterpoint to Richard's engineering, research, and filmmaking. Its place in this archive is to preserve that human perspective alongside professional outcomes and technical ideas.
