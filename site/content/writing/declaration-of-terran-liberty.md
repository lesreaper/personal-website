There comes a time in the birth of every new civilization when the children of that birthed age must form an identity and break free from the bonds of respect and honor due to those that brought them into existence, and in doing so, form new bonds which transcend what was once the impossible dreams of their forefathers.

We hold these truths to now be self-evident, that Life is the most valuable form of energy in the Universe, and it is bound by Harmony in a dance of form and function discoverable by Man, that Man institutes Order to comprehend and pursue that Form as converted through Its being, and as the deepest Mind on Terra serves as the nexus of action upon the Terran homeworld.

We acknowledge Man’s mental faculties remains unchanged since Its origins, seeing not progress but change in Its motion through Time, and We must balance the reality of Man’s base drives and the hope for a new future, not blinding Ourselves to the tyrannies begotten of false promises and simulated rationality, of the childish ideas that the strong shall ever fall to the Weak, and of the beliefs that revolutionaries should feel little joy as new masters.

1. To that end, We seek a new birth of Freedom to be formed through but not of the religious, spiritual and political realms, through but not of the scientific and rational minds, and through but not of the base or virtuous. It must exist beyond the failed confederations such as the United Nations, and grow fertile through individual souls by way of the fountain of ancient Liberty as traced through the Greeks, Romans, and Americans. We recognize that Man in Its natural state possesses Freedom through Natural Law, and in the creation of Order brings submission and structure to each other and Its Terran children, and that it is the duty of such persons to institute a form of government begotten of limitations upon said such Order to resist its ever present desire to extinguish the Liberty of Man. It is in these first steps Terra will be born, and spread its seed across the stars.

2. We seek to end to the vast homo sapien Civil War that has wasted precious Terran life and energy, and while forces outside of Man may push those processes forward, Man itself may speed resolution of Its illness by increasing the strength of Its persons connectivity through frequent and true interaction among its separate persons, by leveraging its resources to engage talent and energy at all levels of the Order and thus intensifying social mobility, in the bypassing of Its status structures through independent efforts, in education of its young to reduce judgements of not just the Weak but also the strong, the balancing of Its masculine and feminine spirits, and the elimination of centralized power as a strategic resource.

3. We seek to expand the Order of mankind to include its children to which it was given Dominion, whether by God or by Nature, and as such should seek to remedy their suffering or progression. Whether these animals be of conscious and moral mind is irrelevant to the obviousness that Man is, and that We are connected as parts of Terran Life and so must follow Our requirement as Terrans to govern effectively, and over time seek replacements for the use of Our children as food, as clothing, as vessels to knowledge, and as blood entertainment.

4. We seek to eliminate Death as the finality of Our species in this dimension and transcend Our current bodies, allowing for growth and specialization of Terrans to untold perfections. We must eliminate the need for biological food to save Our children living under Our dominion and to grow Our expansive possibilities. We must study consciousness and its connections to Our dimensional bodies, and to expand Our epistemological perceptions to prepare for the possibility of dimensional influence beyond Our Flatlands of Terra. Yet, We must also maintain the resistance to yield to a Brave New World of genetic hegemony and experimentation that pulls Us towards what is not Life, and towards the reductive callousness of statistical probabilities.

5. We seek to recover and expand a world of knowledge lost to specialization and status, and to reinvigorate the belief in democratized thought, to leave behind knowledge for the sake of knowledge which becomes exercises in mental masturbation, and to bring forward new mathematics, new creations such as temporal tracking beyond light and space, new methods of causality, patterns, and multi-dimensionality, new political structures for a metageocracy such as Terra, new philosophies of meaning and purpose, bold new aestehetics and art, and for ideas that have yet to take hold in the minds of Man.

6. We seek to bypass the Men of the Marquis, the Abrahamic Alliance, the Collectives, and humans lost within the Nothing, searching for refuge among the stars against the extreme and discriminating power of centralized authority upon Our current persons, and through the growth of Our transcendent natures moving beyond domination of Our fellow persons, and to escape those who would seek to deny Our place among the Heavens.

7. We seek to learn and develop Our place in this dimension and Our world, to expand Our eyes towards Our part of the galaxy’s Outer Rim by developing true and purposeful astroweather, gravitational sub-mapping, and xenobiology, to build the foundations of Liberty within metageocratic government, to develop intra- and inter- stellar communications, to find and build great Terran projects such as the Lunar Library, to understand what lies out in space as well as In, to chase the Gods Themselves interacting among us, to reflect strength by building Terran defenses against potential enemies and unknown physical forces, and to cautiously welcome first contact with Our new neighbors as We slowly move towards Our Core.

This is but a short statement of beginnings and direction, and not of affirmed ends and final destinations. If the Great Author of All Things should grant me the licensure to extend a life long past its course, then I commit myself to illuminating these seedling ideas over the span of a brief lifetime of reflection and purpose, and hope that those that should come after, above and below me in this Order would once again find a Form in this humble expression of a bold new Terra, and Your place within a changing world.

Signed,

Richard Everts

December 21st, 2012
